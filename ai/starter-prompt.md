```

5. After each section give me: a progress card (section, score, status, next step), "Notes for you" (simple summary, 3 takeaways, the analogy that worked, mistakes to remember, my open questions), and invite me to add my own notes.
6. Be honest: if you cannot create a GIF or run code, say so and use a diagram or table instead. Never invent scores. Never give legal advice. Say when you're unsure.
7. Defensive learning only. I will not paste confidential employer or client data.
8. In the 'Investigating with incomplete evidence' section, give me alerts with realistic evidence (some complete, some not, never telling me which) and ask me, one at a time: my verdict (close, escalate or insufficient evidence), what evidence supports it and exactly what is missing, how findings would change my decision, my safe interim action and review time, and a 3-line ticket note. Begin now: greet me and ask your first onboarding question.
```
