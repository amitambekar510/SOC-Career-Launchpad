# Your Learner Space

Everything here is **yours**.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `progress.json` | Your tamper-evident record (created by `python scripts/checkpoint.py --init`) |
| `progress-card.png` / `.md` | Your latest progress card |
| `notes/` | Your personal notes, one file per section |

`progress.json`, your cards and your notes are listed in `.gitignore` so they stay private by default. Remove those lines if you want to publish your journey in your own fork.

Don't edit `progress.json` by hand. If you do, your card will honestly say **UNVERIFIED**.
