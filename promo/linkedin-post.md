## ✅ Main post (copy and paste)

Everyone says "get into cybersecurity."
Almost nobody shows you where to actually start.

So I built **SOC-Career-Launchpad**: a free, open-source, plain-English guide to becoming a SOC analyst (Intern → L1), with an **AI tutor mode**.

What makes it different:

🧠 **The AI asks first.** After each section, your AI assistant quizzes you, waits for your answer, and only then unlocks the next topic. No skipping, no copy-pasting answers.

🎞️ **See it, don't just read it.** Animated GIFs, charts and an interactive lab for the Kill Chain, the life of an alert, the CERT-In 6-hour clock and more.

📇 **A progress card after every section**, plus notes written for you.

🗺️ **A full path:** networking, Linux, Windows & Active Directory, attacks, SIEM, EDR, detection, incident response, cloud security, plus a home-lab guide and an 8-week study plan.

🇮🇳 **Regulations made simple:** CERT-In, SEBI CSCRF, RBI, DPDP, alongside ISO 27001, NIST CSF and MITRE ATT&CK.

It's built for freshers, career switchers (sysadmin, helpdesk, developers), interns and new L1 analysts, and for mentors who want a simple onboarding path.

The goal isn't to hand you answers. It's to help you think like an analyst, so on Day 1 you're ready to be the first line of defence.

👉 Link in the comments. If you're starting out, or you mentor someone who is, I'd love your feedback and contributions.

## 🧭 Before you post (quick checklist)

- [ ] The repository is **public** and the link opens. (If not yet, push it first, then post.)
- [ ] Try the AI tutor mode once in **the AI tool you plan to name** and confirm it asks the questions first. The project is designed for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini, but you should only name tools you have tried.
- [ ] Enable GitHub Pages if you want the interactive Visual Lab link live (Settings → Pages → GitHub Actions).
- [ ] Read the regulations page's "last verified" date and make sure you're comfortable with it.
- [ ] No employer names, client data or screenshots of real systems in the post.

## 📈 Posting tips

- Upload the GIF as the post's image. Keep the link in the **first comment**, since links inside the post body can reduce reach on some feeds.
- Post on a weekday morning in your audience's time zone, then stay around for the first hour to reply to comments.
- Ask one clear question to invite replies, for example "What was the hardest part of starting out in security for you?"
- Tag mentors, communities or friends who teach security. Ask permission first.
- Keep to 3 to 6 hashtags.
- Say honestly what it is: an open learning project with an AI-tutor mode. Don't claim certifications, job guarantees or user numbers.
- Square version for mobile-first feeds or a PDF carousel of the guide pages can be made on request.
