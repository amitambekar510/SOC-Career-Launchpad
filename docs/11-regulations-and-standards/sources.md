# Sources and Further Reading

Last verified: **22 October 2026**. These are the official or primary pages to confirm details. This guide paraphrases them in simple language and is not legal advice.

| Topic | Where to check |
|---|---|
| CERT-In Directions (28 April 2022) | CERT-In website: https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp (also see the CERT-In FAQs) |
| SEBI CSCRF (20 August 2024) and later clarifications / extensions | SEBI website, Circulars section: https://www.sebi.gov.in |
| RBI Cyber Security Framework in Banks (2016) | RBI website: https://www.rbi.org.in |
| RBI Master Direction, IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (2023) | RBI website: https://www.rbi.org.in |
| RBI Master Directions, Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank PSOs (2024) | https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12715 |
| DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 | MeitY website: https://www.meity.gov.in |
| ISO/IEC 27001, 27090, 42001 | ISO catalogue: https://www.iso.org (search by number). Standards are paid documents |
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 | https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework |
| MITRE ATT&CK | https://attack.mitre.org |

## Notes on certainty
- **Dates and thresholds change.** SEBI, for example, extended CSCRF deadlines several times. DPDP obligations phase in until about May 2027.
- **ISO/IEC 27090** was in final publication stages in 2026. Verify its current status on ISO.org.
- Contact details for reporting (email, phone) can change. Use the current ones on cert-in.org.in.
